ABOUT

I’m 🇫🇷🇪🇸🇻🇳, grew up in the 🇳🇱, and worked in 🇹🇳, 🇫🇷, 🇻🇳.

As an Art Director and co-founder of an AI-enhanced digital agency “aaxlo”, I have spent my career shaping pixels, interfaces, and visual identities. My background was forged internationally, fueled by a childhood and professional experiences abroad that sharpened my perspective on alterity and urban space. Now based in Villejuif, I use photography as both a creative release and a return to the tangible world.

Deeply influenced by a lifelong passion for cinema, my first steps behind the lens aimed to simulate film stills -frozen frames capturing a hidden, dramatic narrative within the everyday. Today, my work has structured itself around three specific scopes:

+ Geometric & Cinematic Street Photography: The legacy of my cinematic obsessions, where night-time neons and shadow plays transform the city into a film noir set.

+ Brutalist Cityscape & Minimalist Landscape: An exploration of graphic contrasts, from the raw concrete of the Parisian suburbs to the sun-drenched, minimalist color blocks of Djerba.

+ Environmental Portraiture: Snapshots of life and character captured in their raw authenticity.

While my role as an Art Director naturally pushes me toward perfect, polished compositions, my true ambition lies elsewhere. I refuse to remain just an esthete of the frame: my ultimate goal is to break out of my own visual comfort zone and become a genuinely great street photographer, capable of capturing the accident, the urgency, and the raw truth of humanity.
